feat(audit): show signed and absolute capacity drift are different metrics

`perturbation_capacity_signed_delta` and `perturbation_capacity_delta` differ by
one `abs()`, so it was natural to read them as one metric up to sign. The released
rows say otherwise: the within-setting rank correlation between them has a median
of +0.017, and in 4 of 8 settings their correlation with the target has the
opposite sign. A pairing self-check (`|signed| <= |absolute|` must hold for every
row) passes on all 560 rows, so the split is not a join error: the signed value
averages with sign, letting samples where capacity grew cancel those where it
shrank.

This does not say which metric is better, and it does not close the question of
whether the negative direction is a convention or a denominator effect. It does
mean the two leaderboard entries cannot be cited as two independent confirmations.

Also fixes a real bug found by the new test: `load_panel` dropped NaN rows before
applying repair_rows.csv, deleting exactly the rows the repair exists to fill.
