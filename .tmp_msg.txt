feat(audit): control parameter count in the RQ2 check, and keep the verdict
buckets honest

The early-signal check only controlled trained clean accuracy.  Parameter count is
the more obvious confounder: a bigger architecture can plausibly both drift more
under an attack and train to higher accuracy.  RobBench does not ship parameters,
but for the C=16/N=5 tiny network they follow from the op combination, so they can
be computed rather than estimated (91,226 to 2,699,770 across the 400 rows).

On 400 architectures the parameter count is indeed a real confounder:
rho(target, params) = +0.710 and rho(clean, params) = +0.692.  Controlling it does
not shrink the signal, though -- the best metric (feature_delta) goes from +0.371
under the clean control to +0.422 with both controls (95% interval
[+0.335, +0.490]).  That is a suppressor, not an explanation: the parameter count
removes variation in the metric that has nothing to do with the target, and the
report says so rather than treating a larger coefficient as better evidence.

Verdict buckets: survives 12, shrinks 7, explained_by_capacity 5,
weak_before_capacity 45.  The last bucket exists because the first version counted
metrics that were already below the 0.30 floor as "explained by capacity" -- 47 of
them -- when the honest reading is that they were never strong enough to explain
away.  A test pins the distinction.
